# Growth Roadmap

Date: 2026-06-07

Mode: SaaS growth strategy artifact only. No source files were modified.

## Positioning

Sadhana Hostel SaaS should position as:

"The finance-safe, mobile-first operating system for Indian hostels and PGs."

The market is still full of Excel, registers, WhatsApp follow-ups, scattered screenshots, and manual rent reminders. The product should win by being simpler than enterprise campus platforms and more trustworthy than lightweight rent trackers.

## 0 Customers To 10 Customers

Goal: prove that strangers will pay for the core workflow.

### Product Improvements

- Make onboarding under 30 minutes for one hostel.
- Polish Add Resident -> Invite -> Activation -> Payment Due -> Payment Proof -> Verification.
- Add owner action queue.
- Add resident payment stepper.
- Add complaint/support lifecycle v1.
- Add document/KYC checklist.
- Keep attendance/gate-pass as a clear near-term roadmap item unless needed by first customers.

### Pricing

- Offer simple launch pricing:
  - Starter: one hostel, up to 50 residents.
  - Growth: up to 150 residents, reminders/reports.
  - Pro: multi-hostel, automation, advanced reports.
- Suggested first-customer anchor: monthly subscription plus setup/onboarding fee.
- Avoid per-feature complexity until customers understand value.

### Marketing

- Founder-led sales to hostel/PG owners in Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, and Tamil Nadu.
- Local language demos.
- Before/after narrative: "Stop tracking rent in WhatsApp and notebooks."
- Landing pages by city and use case.
- WhatsApp demo booking CTA.

### Referrals

- Give first 10 customers discounted month for owner referral.
- Ask every converted owner for two nearby hostel owner introductions.

### Retention

- Weekly founder check-ins.
- Daily payment reminder and pending dues report.
- Manual concierge setup for residents, rooms, fees.
- Capture every support issue as product feedback.

## 10 Customers To 100 Customers

Goal: standardize onboarding and reduce founder dependency.

### Product Improvements

- Multi-property owner dashboard.
- Self-serve tenant setup wizard.
- Import residents from CSV.
- WhatsApp reminder templates.
- Attendance/gate-pass v1.
- Maintenance/complaint board.
- Owner daily digest.
- Expense/P&L.
- In-app tutorials for first-run flows.

### Pricing

- Move to tiered pricing by resident count or bed count.
- Add annual plan discount.
- Charge setup fee for data migration.
- Add paid add-ons:
  - WhatsApp messaging pack.
  - Advanced reports.
  - Multi-property.
  - Attendance/gate-pass.

### Marketing

- SEO pages:
  - PG management software India
  - hostel rent collection software
  - hostel attendance management
  - student hostel management software
  - PG payment reminder software
- Publish case studies showing collection improvement and admin time saved.
- Build city-specific landing pages.
- Run WhatsApp/Meta lead ads with demo booking.

### Referrals

- Partner with CA/bookkeepers serving PG owners.
- Partner with local real estate/PG consultants.
- Referral reward: one month free or cash incentive.

### Retention

- Monthly business review email.
- Owner health score.
- Churn warning based on low login/low reminder usage.
- Support SLA.
- Help center and onboarding videos.

## 100 Customers To 1000 Customers

Goal: scale product, operations, support, and acquisition.

### Product Improvements

- Multi-tenant billing/subscription management.
- Role-based staff operations at scale.
- API/integration layer.
- Device integrations for attendance.
- Parent/guardian notification module.
- Advanced automation/rules engine.
- AI assistant only after workflows are stable.
- Robust audit logs and compliance exports.

### Pricing

- Clear public pricing with self-serve trial.
- Annual contracts for larger hostels/institutions.
- Enterprise tier for colleges/universities.
- Usage-based messaging and storage add-ons.

### Marketing

- Category ownership in India:
  - "Hostel management software for Indian PG owners"
  - "Student hostel payment and attendance software"
- Partner program.
- Webinars with hostel owners.
- YouTube demos in English, Telugu, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil.
- Comparison pages against Excel/WhatsApp and leading tools.

### Referrals

- Customer ambassador program.
- City partner/reseller model.
- Referral tracking dashboard.

### Retention

- Customer success team.
- Onboarding playbooks.
- Quarterly product adoption review.
- Health score alerts.
- In-app customer feedback and NPS.
- Dedicated migration support for larger accounts.

## Growth Metrics

| Stage | North Star | Supporting Metrics |
|---|---|---|
| 0-10 | Paying hostels onboarded | Demo-to-paid, setup time, payment workflow success |
| 10-100 | Active hostels retained | Monthly active owners, reminders sent, payments verified |
| 100-1000 | Net revenue retention | Churn, expansion, support SLA, CAC payback |

## Final Recommendation

Do not scale marketing before the first 10 customers can complete resident setup, payment collection, and daily owner follow-up with minimal help. After that, the fastest growth lever is local proof plus WhatsApp-first sales.
